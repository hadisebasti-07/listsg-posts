How much does ONE pilates class REALLY cost in Singapore? 🧘‍♀️💸

Anywhere from S$18 to S$200. Same reformer. It's all in how you pay:

1️⃣ Drop-in class: S$45–60
2️⃣ Private 1-on-1: S$80–200+
3️⃣ Class pack: S$18–55 per class (bigger pack = cheaper)
4️⃣ Unlimited plan: ~S$378 a month

The unlimited test: S$378 ÷ 12 classes = S$31.50 a class. Only going 4 times? S$94.50 a class. 😬

3 ways to pay less:
✅ Drop in first, commit second
✅ Do the unlimited maths (be honest!)
✅ Compare studios and prices upfront on list.sg
