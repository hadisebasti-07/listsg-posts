How much do swimming lessons REALLY cost for kids in Singapore? 🏊‍♀️💸

Anywhere from S$25 to S$110 a lesson:

1️⃣ ActiveSG pool group: S$25–35
2️⃣ Swim-school group: S$30–50
3️⃣ Semi-private (2–3 kids): S$35–45 per child
4️⃣ Private 1-to-1: S$70–110

4 private lessons a month at S$90 = S$360. Per child. 😮 (Plus SwimSafer test fees of about S$80–90 a stage.)

3 ways to pay less:
✅ Share a coach with a sibling or friend
✅ Start with group lessons at a public pool
✅ Compare swim coaches and prices upfront on list.sg
