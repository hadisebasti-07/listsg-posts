Ages 3–5: 5 enrichment classes worth it (and what they cost in Singapore) 🎨🩰🎶

1️⃣ Pre-ballet / dance: S$25–45 a class (from about age 3)
2️⃣ Gymnastics: S$25–45 a lesson (from age 3)
3️⃣ Music class: S$45–60 an hour
4️⃣ Art class: S$45–60 an hour
5️⃣ Swimming: S$25–35 a group lesson

Watch for the extras: registration fees (about S$50–120), uniforms and exam fees. Community club classes are usually cheaper. 💡
