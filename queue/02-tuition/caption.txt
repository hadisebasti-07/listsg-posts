How much does tuition REALLY cost in Singapore? 📚💸

Singapore families spent S$1.8 BILLION on tuition in 2023. Here's what an hour costs in 2026 (1-to-1):

1️⃣ Part-time tutor: S$25–60
2️⃣ Full-time tutor: S$40–90
3️⃣ Ex-MOE teacher: S$55–150
4️⃣ Group class at a centre: from ~S$30/hr

1.5 hrs a week with an ex-MOE PSLE tutor = ~S$480 a month. Per subject. 😮

3 ways to pay less:
✅ Match the tutor to the need (save ex-MOE rates for exam years)
✅ Try a small group or centre class
✅ Compare tutors and rates upfront on list.sg
