How much does a personal trainer REALLY cost in Singapore? 💸

Anywhere from S$50 to S$350 a session. Same hour, 7× the price.

1️⃣ New / freelance trainer: S$50–100
2️⃣ Gym-chain trainer: S$80–150 (+ membership)
3️⃣ Experienced coach: S$120–160
4️⃣ Private studio / specialist: S$160–350+

Twice a week at S$120 = S$960 a month 😮

3 ways to pay less:
✅ Buy a package (usually 10–20% cheaper per session)
✅ Train with a freelancer at an ActiveSG gym
✅ Compare trainers and rates upfront on list.sg
