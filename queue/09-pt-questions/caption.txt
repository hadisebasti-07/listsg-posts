Hiring a personal trainer? Ask these 5 questions BEFORE you pay 🏋️‍♀️

1️⃣ Are you certified? 🚩 Red flag: nothing to show
2️⃣ Can I do a trial? 🚩 Red flag: hard sell into a big package
3️⃣ How will we track progress? 🚩 Red flag: "just trust me"
4️⃣ What if I cancel? 🚩 Red flag: vague answers
5️⃣ Have you trained people like me? 🚩 Red flag: one plan for everyone

A good trainer welcomes these questions. If they get defensive, keep looking.
