How much does a 1-hour massage REALLY cost in Singapore? 💆‍♀️💸

Anywhere from S$45 to S$185++. Same hour, 4× the price:

1️⃣ Neighbourhood parlour: S$45–65
2️⃣ Chain spa: S$70–90
3️⃣ Boutique spa: S$80–150
4️⃣ Hotel spa: S$185++ (before service charge and GST)

Once a month at a hotel spa = S$2,220 a year. At a neighbourhood parlour? S$660. Same 12 hours. 😮

3 ways to pay less:
✅ Book on weekdays
✅ Ask about packages and memberships
✅ Compare massage therapists and prices upfront on list.sg
