Just moved to Singapore? 5 ways to make friends (no apps needed) 🇸🇬👋

1️⃣ Join a run club: FREE at most clubs
2️⃣ Try bouldering: S$18–35 a day pass
3️⃣ Take a dance class: drop-ins from S$17
4️⃣ Do a pottery trial: S$45–98
5️⃣ Book a cooking class: S$70–120 per person

The trick? Pick something you'll go back to every week. Same faces, same time = friends. 🫶
